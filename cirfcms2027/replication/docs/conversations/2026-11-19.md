## 2. Certificate paper (journal, complete draft)
*What a monitoring agent is entitled to conclude: identifiability limits for fault diagnosis in laser powder bed fusion*

**What it covers:** diagnosis as a hypothesis test that returns the set of fault causes consistent with an observation, with exact statistical coverage (the "certificate"). Its results:
- The 10° separation floor used in the literature amounts to assuming a signal-to-noise ratio of 28.1.
- At one instant, three instruments name a unique cause in only 3.5% of builds; with build context that rises to 95.3%.
- The power–gas confound is a theorem, and deliberate probing cannot remove it.
- It includes a benchmark for scoring diagnostic AI agents against the certificate.

**What we did:** built it entirely in this thread. That covers the statistical core, the case generator, the agent backends and scoring, the probing analysis, the results pipeline, a claims checker (76 of 76 numbers), theory checks (32 of 32) and a 32-page draft with seven appendices.

## 3. Fidelity paper (journal, first full draft)
*Validated for prediction, unverified for diagnosis: how accurate a digital twin must be to attribute process faults*
