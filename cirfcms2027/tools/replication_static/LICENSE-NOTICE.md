the default applies: the material may be read, but copying, modification and
redistribution require the authors' permission.

Before the package is shared publicly, the authors should replace this file with
a licence. A common arrangement for research software and its accompanying text
is a permissive software licence (for example MIT or BSD-3-Clause) for `code/`
and `scripts/`, and a Creative Commons licence (for example CC BY 4.0) for
`papers/`, `docs/` and `data/`. The published measurements in `data/input/`
remain subject to the terms of the articles they are taken from, which are cited
there.
